# Screening rule — corneal laser refractive surgery corpus (1988–2025)

KEEP a record if corneal laser refractive surgery is a SUBJECT of the work. Corneal laser refractive surgery means:
PRK and other surface ablation (LASEK, epi-LASIK, transepithelial PRK), LASIK (microkeratome or femtosecond flap),
lenticule extraction (SMILE, ReLEx, FLEx, KLEx, SmartSight, CLEAR), wavefront/topography-guided or custom excimer
ablation, laser presbyopia correction (PresbyLASIK, PRESBYOND, IntraCOR), and excimer/femtosecond laser ablation of
the cornea for refractive purposes. "Subject of the work" includes: outcomes, techniques, complications (haze, dry eye,
flap problems, infection, ectasia after refractive surgery and its treatment), candidate screening and selection for
refractive surgery (including keratoconus screening framed as refractive-surgery candidate screening), basic science
or laboratory studies of excimer/femtosecond corneal ablation and its wound healing, laser engineering for refractive
ablation, comparisons of laser refractive surgery with other refractive procedures (phakic IOL, RLE, orthokeratology),
management of eyes that previously had laser refractive surgery (e.g. IOL power calculation after LASIK/PRK, cataract
or keratoplasty in post-LASIK eyes), surveys/economics/regulation/patient perception of laser refractive surgery,
and letters, comments, editorials or news items about any of these.

EXCLUDE if: laser refractive surgery is mentioned only in passing (background sentence, exclusion criterion, one item
in a list) while the work is about something else; the laser procedure is non-refractive (phototherapeutic keratectomy
for corneal disease, femtosecond keratoplasty, femtosecond cataract surgery, retinal or glaucoma laser, CXL alone);
the record is unrelated to ophthalmology (e.g. "Lasek" as a surname or place, "SMILE" as an acronym for something else,
PRK as a protein kinase, excimer lasers in dermatology/cardiology/materials); or it is non-laser refractive surgery
only (radial keratotomy, ICL, RLE, intracorneal rings) with no laser refractive surgery as a subject.

When genuinely borderline, KEEP and say why in the reason.
